What is adaptOrDie?

adaptOrDie is a time-boxed strategic discovery and delivery playbook designed to validate AI-first product hypotheses, orchestrate agentic workflows, and embed continuous learning loops into organisations. It focuses on quick feedback, measurable outcomes, and production-ready integration patterns so that experimentation drives commercial value.

Key elements

  • Sandbox spike environments with measurable KPIs
  • Agentic workflow prototypes and orchestration patterns
  • Data platform readiness and governance checks
  • Integration design and API-first delivery
